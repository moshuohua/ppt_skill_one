# Content rules

## Insight depth is the first requirement

Develop this reasoning chain before designing the page:

```text
verified fact
→ causal mechanism
→ strategic intent
→ control-point or value-pool shift
→ company action
```

Record one credible alternative explanation and one to three observable signals. Mark each important statement as a verified fact, attributed external interpretation, or author inference. Do not present inference as an official claim.

Rework the conclusion unless at least four of these five tests pass:

1. Non-obvious: it goes beyond summarizing the event.
2. Causal: it explains why the change is happening.
3. Strategic: it identifies a control point, value pool, bargaining-power shift, or new bottleneck.
4. Decision-relevant: it changes a priority, resource allocation, product choice, or experiment.
5. Testable: later evidence can strengthen or falsify it.

Depth belongs in the reasoning, not in added prose. Compress the decisive chain into the slide.

## One page, one claim

Use this logic:

```text
one core claim
→ one to three supporting claims
→ data, cases, or credible external analysis
→ business implication when useful
```

Supporting claims must prove the same conclusion. Do not place three unrelated facts next to one another.

## Executive reading test

The reader should understand:

- the core claim within 10 seconds;
- the evidence and implication within 30 seconds.

Use a dominant visual and a small amount of copy. Remove context the presenter can say aloud.

## Title rules

The title must state a judgment, not name a topic. It should normally reveal both the external change and why it matters.

Bad: `Agent行业趋势`

Better: `模型差距缩小后，Agent运行时将成为云厂商新的竞争控制点`

Bad: `大模型市场分析`

Better: `自研模型难以单点领先，云厂商更应争夺Agent平台与客户运行入口`

- Use `Microsoft YaHei`, 24 pt, bold, and `#C7000B`.
- Prefer 50–70 visual characters. Treat this as a warning range, not a reason to pad a strong conclusion.
- Never insert a manual line break. Let PowerPoint wrap naturally only when needed.
- Allow at most two natural lines. Rewrite the title rather than shrinking it.
- Do not force a one-line title into two lines or add filler to reach the preferred range.
- Put no subtitle, date, caveat, evidence boundary, or explanatory small text below the title.
- Size the title region according to the natural line count and vertically center the title between the 30 pt top margin and the body region.

## Copy rules

- Use direct Chinese and short sentences.
- Keep a sentence near 35 Chinese characters; split it before 45.
- Keep visible body copy normally within 140–200 Chinese characters and never above 220. A strong chart or diagram may carry part of this information through concise labels.
- Use at most three list items in any list.
- Use at most three supporting claims on the page.
- Give every subhead a clear judgment.
- Explain a necessary technical term in plain language at first use.
- Delete anything that does not help prove the page's claim.

Avoid vague phrases such as `范式跃迁`, `价值锚点`, `能力飞轮`, `重塑新范式`, and `重新定义行业格局` unless the words carry a precise, explained meaning.

## Compression test

The final page should still let a leader answer four questions in 30 seconds:

- What changed?
- Why is it happening?
- Which control point or economic value moves?
- What should we do differently?

If the page answers only the first question, it is a news summary rather than an insight slide.

## Evidence sentence pattern

Prefer:

> 判断。关键数字或案例。对业务的含义。

Do not stack several qualifications, causes, and consequences into one sentence.
