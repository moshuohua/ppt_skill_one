# Company context

Use this default lens unless the user provides a different company situation.

The company is a cloud-computing provider with:

- cloud infrastructure;
- an Agent development platform and Agent applications;
- a MaaS business;
- self-developed models whose current results are not industry-leading.

## Questions the slide should help answer

Choose only the questions relevant to the page:

1. What changed outside the company?
2. Which control point or profit pool may move?
3. What does the change mean for cloud, Agent platform, MaaS, model adaptation, industry solutions, or customer delivery?
4. What can the company own even without the best base model?
5. What should management watch, prioritize, stop, or test next?

Do not mention every business line mechanically. Connect the external evidence to the one implication that matters most.

## Reasoning guardrails

- Do not assume the in-house model is leading.
- Do not treat more model training as the default answer.
- Consider infrastructure, Agent runtime, platform entry points, multi-model support, session and state ownership, industry know-how, and delivery capability.
- Distinguish a market fact from the slide author's inference.
- Prefer a useful trade-off or decision over a generic opportunity statement.
